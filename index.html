(03) — AI Lab

PROMPTED, NOT TEMPLATED.

Disclosure: made with AI

These two motion loops were built by prompting an AI. I didn't keyframe them or write the code by hand. I wrote the direction, judged every version, and kept pushing until the result matched what I saw in my head. That direction is the skill, and it's what I'm showing here.

Why prompting is a design skill

⌘

Direction into words

I turn a picture in my head into precise instructions: palette, type, layout, motion timing, and what loops versus what stays still.

↻

Iterate like an art director

The first output is never the final. I review each pass, name exactly what's off, and steer until it's right.

◎

On-brand, every time

The real product, the real colours, the real claims. AI output only matters if a brand can actually use it.

⚡

Hours, not weeks

Pieces that would normally need an animator and a developer, delivered as one person with a sharp brief.
